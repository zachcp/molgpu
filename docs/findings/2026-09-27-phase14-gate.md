## Acceptance evidence

- The independent PDB2PQR `--ff=AMBER` 1crn fixture matches every template
  heavy-atom charge after hydrogen folding within `1e-4` e. An internal ALA O
  matches the source's `-0.5679` e. All 1crn standard residue nets are integral.
- No standard polymer atom is unmatched on 1crn, 1a4y, 1tqn, 4c7r or 1bna. DNA
  names and water aliases resolve. The 4c7r chloride ion receives `-1` e from
  the cited CCD ion table. The named 1tqn chain gap is reported without creating
  an internal terminus.
- On 1ejg, a THR OG1 with HG1 keeps `-0.6761` e, while a THR OG1 without HG1
  receives its `+0.4102` e for `-0.2659` e. Active model/conformer residue sums
  are checked on 1ejg and 2k39. Missing template heavy atoms are reported as
  incomplete residues rather than silently normalised; their observed residue
  sums can be fractional.
- Ethanol, acetic acid, dimethylamine, benzene and acetate fixtures match RDKit
  2025.09.2 heavy-atom plus implicit-H charges within `1e-3` e. Tests cover
  unknown order, missing parameters, unsupported valence, modified polymer,
  polymer-linked component, template exclusion and a glycan-like two-residue
  connected component. The 1tqn heme is explicitly refused because Fe has no
  supported parameter.
- The package README, API snapshot, changelog, design and roadmap now state the
  charge contract and the dual MIT/BSD-3-Clause license. Generated source
  includes the verbatim PDB2PQR license and pinned commit; its exact source
  inputs and a regeneration script are checked in.
