## PR #15 review (1to.12–1to.17)

Review of the PR found that real mmCIF ligands took an untested path: io
supplies `chem_comp_bond` Kekulé orders with an aromatic flag, and Gasteiger
turned every flagged bond into order 4 and counted it as 1.5 for implicit-H
valence. Against RDKit 2026.03.6, fused aromatics (naphthalene, indole, adenine,
so ATP/NAD/FAD) and furan were refused, and pyrrole, imidazole and thiophene
were charged 0.06–0.09 e wrong without a report. Hybridization from bond orders
alone also refused sulfonamides and mis-charged phosphate P and sulfoxide S.
Fixes: Kekulé orders are used as given; order-4 bonds are kekulized, refusing an
undetermined ring-hydrogen position; hybridization and conjugation follow
RDKit's rules (RDKit charges sulfoxide and sulfone S as sp3, not with its
`so`/`so2` rows). About 45 molecules, including ATP, HEPES, imatinib, biotin and
charged species, now match RDKit within `1e-3` e on the component-bond path.
Ligands with alternate locations are charged per conformer (1to.14). The ion
table now matches CCD formal charges: FE is Fe(III), IOD is iodide, I (inosinic
acid) is removed and other common ions were added; ions with alternate locations
are charged (1to.15). Fully template-covered components such as water are no
longer reported as refusals (1to.16). Charged carbon uses the isoelectronic
valence (1to.17).
