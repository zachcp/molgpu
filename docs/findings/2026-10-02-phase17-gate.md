# Phase 17 gate: stateful dynamics (ahc.9)

Close-out audit for the `ahc` epic. The contract is in
[the plan](2026-10-02-stateful-dynamics-plan.md). Its counter-review section
records the 13 ahc.2 findings with their verdicts, and its implementation notes
record where the build departed from the plan, with evidence. The evidence below
comes from `deno task test`, `deno task test:dynamics:gpu`,
`deno task test:components` (`packages/viewer/test/run-elastic.mjs`) and
`deno task test:site`. All were run on 2026-10-02 on an Apple silicon Mac with
Chrome WebGPU (Metal). CI runs the same suites under SwiftShader. The dynamics
suite is in the `efield` group of `scripts/run-webgpu-ci.sh`; `run-elastic.mjs`
and the site are in the `viewer` and `site` groups.

Two acceptance criteria were amended after measurement, and both amendments are
recorded on ahc.6 and in plan §5. The 2 Å linear-response oracle cannot hold for
distance springs: at T = 0 the fitted error is 23 %, against 1.4 % at 0.1 Å. A
single 5e3-step kinetic window on 1crn's 46 nodes scatters by 1.4 %.

## Measured cost

| System                       | Nodes  | Springs | Steps/s | Per frame at 20 steps |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | ------- | ------- | --------------------- |
| 1crn (`run-elastic`, 1e5)    | 46     | 688     | ~40 000 | < 1 ms                |
| 4c7r (`run-elastic`, 1e5)    | 1 534  | 48 120  | ~19 000 | ~1 ms                 |
| 8 × 4c7r CA (100k-atom size) | 12 272 | 384 960 | ~1 500  | ~13 ms                |

The plan estimated about 4 ms per frame at 100k atoms, from bandwidth alone. The
measurement is about 3× that. Four dependent dispatches per step, one of them a
single-workgroup reduction, probably make it latency-bound rather than
bandwidth-bound; this was not profiled. The default `maxStepsPerFrame` (20)
therefore fills most of a 60 Hz frame at that size, and `status.lagging` reports
when it falls behind. Fusing dispatches is filed as a follow-up rather than
tuned here.

## Invariants

- **INVARIANT 2:** `@molgpu/dynamics` exports WGSL strings, typed arrays and
  plain data (`langevinWgsl`, `langevinBuffers`, `elasticDisplacementWgsl`,
  `elasticNetworkData`); no `@use-gpu` import below the viewer. Hardening H1–H6
  pass for every package, and `api.txt` is updated.
- **INVARIANT 4:** a `<Spacefill>` colour edit under a running provider costs
  zero upload bytes, zero geometry builds and zero integrator batches
  (`run-elastic`). The integrator buffers upload once per network.
- **INVARIANT 6:** `<ElasticNetwork>` publishes through `CoordinateKernel`, with
  one output row per upstream row and unmapped rows copied. A network built for
  a different atom count throws. Step 0 equals upstream within 1e-5 Å.
- **TimelineContext contract and CONCEPT 4/8:** nothing advances from the frame
  loop. A lagging provider re-renders itself only to reach a target the
  application set; caught up or paused, it requests no repaint. With
  checkpoints, a `step` curve on the global timeline scrubs.
- **Publication and lifetime (crj.7, retirement decision):** the first
  coordinates are `ready: false`. The provider submits its own encoder before
  the frame's compute pass, and it releases ownership of its buffers instead of
  destroying them. Extending the run-retirement memory matrix to it is ahc.11.
