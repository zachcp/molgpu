Impostors are real spheres. Pick Impostor proof: the seams where spheres overlap are curved sphere–sphere intersection arcs, which is only possible if the fragment shader writes per-fragment depth. Flat billboards would hard-occlude with circular edges.
depth picks the size space. depth:1 is world-space and perspective-correct — atoms keep their Ångström radius as you zoom. depth:0 is pixels, so atoms stay the same on-screen size regardless of distance. Molecular work needs depth:1.
Why not real sphere geometry? Cost, measured in this spike:
pathCPU buildbytes/atom
impostor0.9 ms @ 100k16
merged mesh593 ms @ 20k~3 900
~200× the memory, extrapolating to ~3.2 GB at 1M atoms. Mol* uses impostors at scale for the same reason.
